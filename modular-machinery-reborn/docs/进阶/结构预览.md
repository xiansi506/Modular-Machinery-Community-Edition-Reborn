# 结构预览 / Structure preview

适用于 **0.120.0 / Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge**。可从蓝图界面或 JEI 的“结构预览”页面查看机器；JEI 中每台机器对应一条结构预览。

0.118.3 修复停留或轮换后材料表全部变成控制器的问题，材料种类和数量会在 JEI 更新显示时恢复。见 [本次修复说明](../归档/版本记录/修复记录-0.118.3.md)。

## 操作

- 在模型区域拖动可旋转或平移，滚轮缩放，中键恢复视角。工具栏可展开，按钮悬停时显示说明。
- 点击模型中的方块可选中并显示轮廓。左侧显示该方块和最多六种可替换材料；有结构修饰符图标时，方块材料会向右错开。
- 在 JEI 中悬停选中方块、候选材料或下方材料格，可使用原生物品提示和 JEI 配方／用途查询，包括默认的 R／U 键及相应点击操作。具体按键以自己的 JEI 设置为准。
- 有动态结构时，用长度按钮增减重复段。下方材料种类和数量随当前长度更新；长度归零后，不再需要的材料会从可见槽位消失。
- 下方最多显示 18 格；材料超过容量时，最后一格显示 `+N`。所有结构候选材料仍可参与 JEI 查找，包括初始长度为零的动态段。
- 切片按钮只显示选定层，选择框也只针对可见方块。成型预览按机器配置隐藏组件，并为临时控制器关联当前机器和成型状态；自定义模型与动画仍需要相应资源及 GeckoLib。
- 水、岩浆和含水方块按流体路径绘制。放大或平移后，视口两侧仍可选择可见方块。

预览不会搭建或修改世界中的机器。方块选择使用单格边界；细杆、不规则模型或超出格子的自定义模型，选择范围可能与可见表面略有差别。

## English

0.118.3 fixes material icons reverting to the controller after JEI ingredient cycling. The current material types and counts are restored when JEI updates its displayed ingredients. See the [fix notes](../归档/版本记录/修复记录-0.118.3.md).

Open a blueprint preview or JEI's **Structure Preview** category. Drag the model to rotate or pan, scroll to zoom, and use the middle button to reset the camera. Expand the toolbar and hover its buttons for their controls.

Click a visible block to select it. Its item and up to six alternatives appear on the left, shifted beside modifier icons when necessary. In JEI, these icons and the lower material grid use native ingredient slots: item tooltips, clicks, and recipe/use lookup follow your JEI key bindings, including the default R/U keys.

Dynamic-length buttons update both the structure and its material counts. Items no longer needed disappear when a section shrinks to zero. The grid has 18 positions; structures with more distinct materials reserve the last position for a `+N` marker. Search indexing includes alternatives and initially absent dynamic sections.

Layer mode limits the view and selection to the chosen layer. Formed preview associates its temporary controller with the machine and formed state, enabling its configured model/animation hooks. GeckoLib models still require the corresponding dependency and resources. Water, lava and waterlogged blocks have a fluid pass. Visible blocks near the viewport sides remain selectable after zooming or panning.

The preview does not build or change the world. Picking uses unit block bounds, so non-cubic or oversized geometry can differ from its selection region.

**Validation:** code, offline regressions and a production build have been checked. No Forge game or OpenGL visual acceptance was performed. Lighting, fluid blending, GeckoLib animations, GUI scaling and native JEI tooltip/query behavior still need gameplay acceptance. Failed model/entity rendering is isolated to the current preview scene and can be retried after rebuilding the scene or reloading resources.
